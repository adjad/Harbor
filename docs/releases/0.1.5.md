# Harbor 0.1.5

An update to Harbor 0.1.0. It reorganises the Brief, adds a two-column Day, closes the browser tabs a Find walk opened, and fixes a set of Brief, Day and study-block problems. Everything in [0.1.0](0.1.0.md) still applies.

## What's new in 0.1.5

### Brief

- **Two zones.** The Brief is grouped into what Harbor has ready for you ("Before you ask", to-dos, school) and what is on your plate (calendar, mail and texts that need attention, things found in your mail). In the two-column Brief, "Found in your mail" sits in the right half.
- **Shorter rows.** A calendar event named in the calendar line is not repeated as a row, a to-do's second line shares the title line when it fits, and the stacked Brief shows two rows per list before "Show all".
- **The Brief is reorganised, not smaller.** It is about the same height as before.

### Day

- **Two columns.** The header's two-column button now applies to Brief and Day together. In the wide Day, the left half holds notices, the summary, all-day items, the agenda and Old reminders; the right half holds Still to fit and School this week. Ask, Meet and Study stay narrow.
- **Switching tabs is one motion.** Leaving or entering the wide Brief or the wide Day no longer snaps the panel width in the first frame.

### Find course materials

- **Harbor closes the tabs it opened.** After a walk, Harbor closes only the browser tabs it opened itself. macOS asks once per browser for permission to control it. If you decline, or the browser does not answer, Harbor leaves the tabs open, says so on the card, and does not ask again during that walk.
- **Stop answers at once,** and the card shows what was found so far.
- **Stricter refusals.** Every redirect is checked against the never-open rules, course and assignment addresses must be on the page's own site, and form, poll, payment and meeting sites are refused.

### Ask

- **Class notes.** "Summarise the class notes on…" also searches the slides you added in Study, not only Apple Notes.

## Fixed

- **Group messages** read "Mom in a group of 3 (…)" when the group has no name, instead of leaving a gap.
- **The Brief's calendar line** no longer says "Nothing else on your calendar today" with nothing above it, and it counts all-day events.
- **A to-do's quoted line** is hidden when it only repeats the title. A course code at the end of a title moves to the line below, and long course names wrap.
- **The Day summary** counts from the same list as the agenda.
- **A Still-to-fit reason** is no longer cut off at its number.
- **Study blocks.** Undo refuses, and cancels nothing, if you changed the event's start, end or title in Calendar. A block you deleted in Calendar stops showing as added, and Day offers "Add to calendar" again.

## Requirements

- A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later). This download is for Apple silicon only.
- macOS 14 or newer. The built-in engine needs macOS 26.2 or newer; on macOS 14 to 26.1, Harbor needs another local engine running on the Mac (oMLX, Ollama, LM Studio or any OpenAI-compatible server).
- 16 GB of memory, and about 8 GB of free disk for the first model download.
- For Study material: your own OpenRouter or Gemini API key. For slides read page by page: a Gemini API key.
- For Gmail, Calendar and Drive: a Google account on the app's test-user list.

## Before you install

- **Harbor is not notarized.** macOS blocks the first open; allow it in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.
- **Quit any other assistant build first.** Harbor keeps its data in `~/.moe` and its service on the local port 8765 (the built-in engine uses 8000). Another build using the same folder or port will conflict with it.
- **Google sign-in is in testing mode.** Only Google accounts on the test-user list can sign in, Google shows an "unverified app" warning, and sign-ins expire after 7 days.
- **Slides page reading needs Study's own connector** set to the Gemini API with a Study key. Harbor's other cloud settings do not turn it on.

## What was and was not tested

- **Automated checks:** the full release build, with its Simulation QA gates, ran on the tagged commit before this was published.
- **Reviewed independently:** tab closing and its declined-permission path, the Find refusal changes, and the study-block changes.
- **Not reviewed independently:** the faster Stop, and the move of Find's rules into their own file. Both are covered by their own checks.
- **Not tried when these notes were written:** tab closing against a real browser, and the new Brief and two-column Day on live data.
- **Not tried:** the Gemini handles everything switch with pictures and voice.
- **Not tried:** any Intel Mac.

## Known issues

- **The two-column button itself** snaps the panel width when you press it.
- **With Reduce Motion on,** the panel is briefly the wrong height (about 24 points) in four places: a Day notice appearing or clearing, closing meeting notes, and a Study writing job finishing.
- **A suggested block and a study block** can start at the same minute in Day.
- **If Calendar moves an event to a new time and re-issues it,** a study block can be booked twice.
- **The Brief's filter and sparkle buttons** show when there are no cards, and the footer's "found N things" can disagree with what is visible.
- **A deck left reading** shows "Reading slides…" until Study is refreshed.
- **Lecture recordings are links only.** Harbor does not open or read them.
- **Teacher-added external links inside Modules are refused.**
- **Find's never-open rule is strict.** It also refuses study material whose title contains words like "midterm" or "final".
- **Find needs a course's Canvas address.** For a course without one, open the course page and press "Read again" first.
- **Recent fixes to cloud-key handling** are covered by tests that stand in for the Keychain. They have not been tried by hand with every key and macOS prompt.
- **Some features are untried in the running app:** reading email and Messages attachments, meeting notes and writing-style drafts.
- **Study reads Canvas only.** Other school sites are not supported. Treat Study as untested against school sites and Drive files you have not tried yourself.
- **Study has no Google account picker.** It uses the first connected account that allowed Drive access.
- **Forwarding through Apple Mail is not available yet.** Harbor says plainly that nothing was sent.
- **Reading the Mac's Mail and Messages** needs Full Disk Access in some cases.
- **Harbor does not update itself.**
