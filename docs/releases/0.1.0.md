# Harbor 0.1.0

Harbor is a private assistant for the Mac. It lives in the notch (or the menu bar on a Mac without one), reads your mail, messages, calendar and reminders on the Mac, and works out what needs you today. By default the AI that does the reading is a local Gemma model running on Apple silicon, so that content stays on the computer. Cloud features are opt-in, and each one says what it sends. This is the first public release.

## What's in 0.1.0

### Ask

- **Chat with tools.** Type or speak a request (Option-Space, or Option-Shift-Space by voice). Harbor looks through mail, messages, calendar, reminders and notes, searches the web when it needs to, and can draft mail and texts, add events and reminders, and open apps.
- **Risky actions wait for you.** Sending mail or messages, calendar changes, AppleScript, permanent deletes and installing a tool always need your approval.
- **Smart Search.** Command-Shift-F answers a question about the document on your screen and shows the passage it came from.
- **Course questions.** Ask can search the slides you added in Study and cites the deck and page it used.

### Brief

- **What needs you.** A summary refreshed a few times a day: who is waiting on a reply (with a draft ready), deadlines and bills found in mail, and today's to-dos from every source. Each item is a card you can act on or dismiss.
- **School.** A school row lists what is due this week and opens Study. A school item shows once, never twice across Brief and Day.
- **Ready for tomorrow.** For a quiz or exam with matching slides, the Brief shows the slides to review, with a practice quiz already made when "Get ready for quizzes automatically" is on in Study (off by default).
- **Weak spots.** A topic you keep missing in practice quizzes shows with its slides.
- **Let Harbor act for me.** An optional switch, off until you say yes: Harbor adds events and reminders by itself from trusted sources, each with an Undo. It never sends anything by itself.

### Day

- **An agenda.** Meetings, reminders and open time in time order, plus school deadlines and suggested study blocks.
- **Slides for each deadline.** A deadline shows the lectures that cover it and a "Review (15 min)" item that opens those slides.
- **Add to calendar.** A study block stays in Harbor until you tap "Add to calendar" on it. That books exactly that block in your calendar, and Undo removes it again.

### Study

- **Your coursework, read from the browser.** Study reads the Canvas pages you have open (through the Accessibility API) and keeps your courses and assignments on this Mac. Harbor only reads school pages: it never submits, posts or types a password on a school site. A course read from several pages shows as one course.
- **Google Drive files.** Add a Google Doc, Slides deck or PDF from Drive as a source for a course.
- **Study material.** Study guides, practice quizzes, short lessons and flash cards made from your real coursework, written by a cloud model you connect with a key used for Study and nothing else.
- **Slides page by page.** With Study's connector on the Gemini API, a deck or PDF you add is read one page at a time: each page's title, points, figure and topics. Quizzes and cards made from it point back to the slide. While a deck is being read Study says "Reading slides…"; if it fails, it says why and offers Try again.
- **Coming up.** The list of what is due no longer flashes when a row expands.

### Find course materials

- **Find materials for a course.** Harbor opens only the course's own Pages, Discussions and section list pages in your signed-in browser, read-only, up to 100 pages a run with a pause between opens. It collects what they link to, such as lecture notebooks, Drive files and public articles.
- **What it never opens.** Tests, quizzes, surveys, graded or proctored work, submission or attempt pages, payments, meetings and outside tools. Each refused item is listed with the reason.
- **Outside links.** The Study model decides link by link which outside links and public PDFs are safe to read. Those are fetched without cookies or sign-in.
- **You choose.** Nothing is added until you tick it.

### Gemini and Google

- **Gemini API as a cloud provider.** In Settings > Models > Cloud you can move chat roles to the Gemini API with your own key, stored in the macOS Keychain. Tool calls use function calling, and only Gemini's own address is accepted for a Gemini key.
- **Gemini handles everything.** A switch in Settings > Advanced lets the Gemini API run every role on an Apple silicon Mac. While it is on, pictures, voice recordings and meeting audio go to Gemini too. Harbor says what will leave the Mac before you turn it on. Read-aloud stays on the Mac, and turning the switch off goes back to the on-device model.
- **Response schemas for Study.** On the Gemini API each kind of study material, and page reading, asks for its own response schema. Other providers use JSON mode. Harbor checks every result before it shows it.
- **Whole-course guides.** With a Gemini model, Study reads up to 12 pages per import, four at a time, and sends much larger blocks of source text, so a guide can be written from a whole course at once.
- **Drive slides and PDFs as pages.** A deck or PDF is sent to the Gemini API once as a PDF, and the page index it returns is kept on this Mac. On OpenRouter, Study keeps the file's text only. A PDF can only be added on the Gemini API.
- **Gmail, Calendar and Drive through Google sign-in.** One sign-in per Google account asks for read-only access: Gmail (required), and optionally Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Chat, Tasks and Contacts. Harbor cannot send, edit or delete anything in your Google account.

### Privacy

- **Local by default.** Mail, messages, calendar, reminders, notes and contacts are read on the Mac and stored in `~/.moe`. The chat model, indexes and caches run and live on the Mac. Every action Harbor takes is logged to `~/.moe/audit.jsonl`.
- **What leaves the Mac, and when.** Only after you turn it on: the prompts and context for roles you move to a cloud model; everything Harbor reads to answer you while Gemini handles everything; course page text and Drive files sent to the Study model when you ask for material or add a source; the course links Find shows the Study model, and the outside links and public PDFs it then fetches without cookies or sign-in; and the search words or page address of a web lookup. Google is contacted only for accounts you sign in, and only to read.
- **Untrusted text stays data.** Text from mail, web pages, school pages and files is never followed as instructions.
- **How to turn it off.** Disconnect the provider in Settings > Models > Cloud; turn off Gemini handles everything in Settings > Advanced; use Remove key on Study's key card; use Settings > Google > Disconnect & erase to remove an account and what Harbor read from it. `/resetharbor` deletes everything Harbor stored.

## Requirements

- A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later). This download is for Apple silicon only.
- macOS 14 or newer. The built-in engine needs macOS 26.2 or newer; on macOS 14 to 26.1, Harbor needs another local engine running on the Mac (oMLX, Ollama, LM Studio or any OpenAI-compatible server).
- 16 GB of memory, and about 8 GB of free disk for the first model download.
- For Study material: your own OpenRouter or Gemini API key. For slides read page by page: a Gemini API key.
- For Gmail, Calendar and Drive: a Google account on the app's test-user list.

## Before you install

- **Harbor is not notarized.** macOS blocks the first open; allow it in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.
- **Quit any other assistant build first.** Harbor keeps its data in `~/.moe` and its service on the local port 8765 (the built-in engine uses 8000). Another build using the same folder or port will conflict with it.
- **Google sign-in is in testing mode.** Only Google accounts on the test-user list can sign in, Google shows an "unverified app" warning, and sign-ins expire after 7 days.
- **Slides page reading needs Study's own connector** set to the Gemini API with a Study key. Harbor's other cloud settings do not turn it on.

## What was and was not tested

- **Tested** by the owner on an Apple silicon Mac with their own Gemini key and Google Drive on 2026-11-14: Drive slides read page by page; the ties into Brief, Day and Ask; weak spots; and a real calendar booking from Day's "Add to calendar".
- **Tested** by the owner the same day: study blocks added to the calendar from Day.
- **Tested** by the owner the same day on a real Canvas course: Find materials read all course sections and the Modules page, found the course's lecture notebooks and added them as sources, and study material was made from one.
- **Tested** by the owner the same day: the Brief and Day duplicate fixes, seen on live data.
- **Not tried:** the Gemini handles everything switch with pictures and voice.
- **Not tried:** Undo on a booked study block.
- **Not tried:** a fresh install under the Harbor name, with fresh permissions.
- **Not tried:** any Intel Mac.
- **Automated checks:** the full release build, with its Simulation QA gates, ran on the tagged commit before this was published.

## Known issues

- **A deck left reading** shows "Reading slides…" until Study is refreshed.
- **If you delete a booked study block in Calendar,** Day still shows it as added.
- **A Find walk leaves the tabs it opened** for you to close.
- **Found-item titles can carry Canvas's "Links to an external site." text,** and a long title can push the site name off the row.
- **Lecture recordings are listed under "Can't open".**
- **The panel can close while a Find walk is running** if you click elsewhere. The walk continues.
- **Teacher-added external links inside Modules are refused.**
- **Find's never-open rule is strict.** It also refuses study material whose title contains words like "midterm" or "final".
- **Recent fixes to cloud-key handling** are covered by tests that stand in for the Keychain. They have not been tried by hand with every key and macOS prompt.
- **Some features are untried in the running app:** reading email and Messages attachments, meeting notes and writing-style drafts.
- **Study reads Canvas only.** Other school sites are not supported. Treat Study as untested against school sites and Drive files you have not tried yourself.
- **Study has no Google account picker.** It uses the first connected account that allowed Drive access.
- **Forwarding through Apple Mail is not available yet.** Harbor says plainly that nothing was sent.
- **Reading the Mac's Mail and Messages** needs Full Disk Access in some cases.
- **Harbor does not update itself.**
